Demo analytics notice

CodeJet uses first-party demo analytics to understand visits to the website and engagement with its video. When enabled, this records the public IP address observed by our website’s front door, server timestamps, page visits, video playback events, and campaign labels included in a link. We also record the referring hostname, when available, and a simple possible-bot flag.

A short-lived identifier stored in your browser tab’s session storage connects these events. The identifier expires after four hours. This tracker does not use tracking cookies, record contact-form contents, or send IP addresses to a company-identification service.

Playback coverage is based on the player’s reported played ranges. It does not establish who watched the video or whether they paid attention. The embedded video is delivered by Cloudflare Stream, which processes video requests separately.

The private analytics report is restricted to CodeJet. Records older than {{ retention }} days are removed during collection, report access, or manual maintenance. Exported reports and hosting backups have separate retention.

This tracker skips collection when the browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal. Browser blocking or network failures may also prevent collection.
