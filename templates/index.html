Why CodeJet

Your data shouldn't require a scavenger hunt.

Retailers have more data than ever. But getting a clear answer can still mean finding the right report, asking an analyst, digging through a spreadsheet, or waiting for someone to build another dashboard. AI can change that — but only when it understands the business behind the data.

The answer exists. Finding it is the problem.

Institutional knowledge is often spread across reports, queries, dashboards, spreadsheets, and the people who know how to interpret them.

Generic AI does not know your business.

It does not inherently know how you define sales, margin, inventory, vendors, locations, reporting periods, or which data a particular user should be allowed to see.

The CodeJet Blueprint

Your business logic becomes the blueprint.

CodeJet takes the logic already embedded in the reporting environment your organization trusts and structures it into a blueprint an AI model can understand.

Instead of asking generic AI to figure out your business, CodeJet gives the model the context, relationships, definitions, and guardrails it needs to work within your environment.

  • Leverage existing reports, queries, schemas, and definitions.
  • Map the metrics and relationships that matter to your organization.
  • Provide users with a consistent way to ask questions in plain English.
  • Return answers in the format that best fits the request.

Ask the Business Question

Ask a business question. Not a database question.

CodeJet is designed to let authorized users interact with retail data naturally while keeping the response grounded in the definitions and logic your company already uses.

Getting the answer is only part of the job. CodeJet can also turn that analysis into the format your team actually needs — from a quick chart or detailed table to an executive PowerPoint or PDF.

AI With Boundaries

AI is more useful when it knows the rules.

CodeJet is designed around controlled access to approved business data and the context required to interpret that data responsibly.

Approved Data Sources

Define the systems and data sets CodeJet is allowed to use when answering questions.

Defined Business Logic

Role-Aware Access
