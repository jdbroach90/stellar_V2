## Short company description

CodeJet is a retail intelligence platform that helps teams ask sales, margin, and inventory questions in plain English using the context in their existing reports and business definitions.

## Extended profile description

CodeJet is retail analytics software built around the reporting knowledge your team already trusts. It structures the context in reports, queries, data models, and business definitions so an AI agent can support plain-English questions about the business.

Approved sources, metric definitions, relationships, and role-aware access define the scope. Outputs can include a summary, table, chart, PowerPoint, or PDF. We work with retail teams as AI partners, starting with a focused reporting question and an agreed way to check the answer against existing reports.

Watch the demo at https://codejet.app/demo, then tell us which recurring question slows your team down.
